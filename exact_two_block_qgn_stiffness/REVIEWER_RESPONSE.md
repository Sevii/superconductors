# Reviewer-covariance revision

## Central correction

The previous draft and verifier twisted the active projector while holding the compact bridge kernel fixed. That convention produces the nonzero derivative reported in the old Eq. (5.4), but the bridge is a multi-site bilinear. Under the displacement-resolved Peierls prescription its kernel must also shift as `B(k+A)`. When `P(A)` and `B(A)` are twisted covariantly,

\[
P(A)B(A)P(A)=U(A)[P(0)B(0)P(0)]U(A)^\dagger,
\]

so a zero projected row remains zero for every `A`. The revised paper withdraws the compact bridge as a physical ordinary-twist source and retains it as an explicit covariance counterexample.

`check_bridge_covariance.py` reproduces both conventions. On the default grid it finds:

- fixed-kernel derivative error relative to the old analytic formula: `1.65e-10`;
- covariantly projected bridge value: `2.81e-16`;
- covariant finite-difference derivative: `1.99e-10`, arising from differencing machine-zero values.

## Structural replacement

The revised draft proves the termwise net-displacement criterion for a neutral many-fermion monomial. A vertex is invariant under every uniform Peierls twist exactly when its covering-lattice net displacement vanishes. The pair-hop

\[
c_{1x\uparrow}^\dagger c_{1x'\downarrow}^\dagger c_{2x'\downarrow}c_{2x\uparrow}
\]

is position balanced and therefore exactly twist invariant.

For `B_X=X+X†` and `B_Y=-i(X-X†)`, the corrected decomposition is

\[
\frac12[g_X^2 B_X^2+g_Y^2 B_Y^2]
=\frac{J}{2}(XX^\dagger+X^\dagger X)
-\frac{P_{ph}}{2}(X^2+X^{\dagger 2}),
\]

with `J=g_X^2+g_Y^2` and `P_ph=g_Y^2-g_X^2`. Thus `P_ph` is precisely the pair-transfer anisotropy, up to the displayed sign convention. Position balance permits `P_ph != 0`; it does not by itself force equal quadratures.

## Conditional Jacobi results retained and strengthened

The many-composition Jacobi matrix is retained as an exact source-Gram reduction conditional on a legitimate covariant microscopic transfer source. It is no longer presented as being generated by the compact filtered bridge.

Two new results are included:

1. **Transfer-dark lemma.** For the bridge-source Gram alone at `n=V`, if either quadrature vanishes, the exact zero vector has components
   \[
   v_r=(-\operatorname{sgn}P_{ph})^r\binom Vr.
   \]
   The numerical residual at `V=120` is `3.8e-15`.

2. **Full middle-filling thermodynamic formula.** For the paper's y-direction base curvatures,
   \[
   \lim_{V\to\infty}\frac{\lambda_{min}(Q_y^{(V)})}{4V}
   =\frac18\min\left\{J,\min(g_X^2,g_Y^2)+\frac94U\right\}.
   \]
   The reviewer's `J/8` improvement is exact in the endpoint-localized regime
   \[
   U\ge \frac49\max(g_X^2,g_Y^2).
   \]
   This condition holds for the verifier parameters `U=1`, `g_X=0.8`, `g_Y=0.3`; the sequence through `V=120` converges to `J/8=0.09125`. The explicit condition avoids claiming `J/8` for arbitrarily weak `U`, where the central-composition branch can remain lower.

## WSe2 reinterpretation

The WSe2 geometry remains useful, but every `Gamma` is now labeled a **geometric screening proxy**, not a demonstrated source coefficient. The old `t0=2 P_ph Gamma` material interpretation has been removed.

The revised discussion distinguishes two facts:

- the published `U,V,J` monomials are position balanced and carry no independent uniform-Peierls phase;
- their actual Fierz tensor gives `P_ph=0` and hence equal quadratures.

A separate position-balanced pair hop can carry `P_ph != 0` without violating gauge covariance, but it has not yet been extracted for the candidate.

## Reproducibility fixes

`interaction_singular_subbundle_scan_revised.py` now:

- finite-differences rank-one projectors rather than phase-aligned singular vectors;
- records the finite-difference step and method;
- defines the max-over-path proxy exactly as
  \[
  \Gamma_i^{max}=\frac1{a_M^2}\sum_{(a,b)}
  \left\langle\operatorname{Tr}[P_r(D_iP_a)^2]\max_\ell
  \left(|\langle Q_r|T_\ell|Q_b\rangle|^2+
  |\langle Q_r|T_\ell^\dagger|Q_b\rangle|^2\right)\right\rangle_k;
  \]
- keeps old `t0` keys only as backward-compatible **conditional proxies**.

The three archived representatives were re-run with projector differences. Their `Gamma` values change by less than roughly `3.3e-8` in absolute value, as expected for these well-isolated points. Repeating the calculation at `delta_frac = 1e-3, 2e-3, 4e-3` changes the proxy by at most `4.92e-7` from the middle-step value.

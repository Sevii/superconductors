# Claim status: microscopic transfer of exact Meissner weight

## Reviewer-safe headline

> The complete weighted-degree-eight audit rules out analytic all-composition transfer of the unmodified matched microscopic family. An explicit finite-range, higher-body counterterm restores the two-sided parent-row ideal through that order, while a strictly overcompensated positive-semidefinite completion selects an exact coherent dark AGP and yields a rigorous finite-coupling zero-frequency transverse Meissner floor. Equality of the total Kohn, Abelian, and Meissner kernels is claimed only for rows satisfying the target-metric hydrodynamic-closure and point-group isotropy hypotheses.

## Analytically established in the merged paper

1. **Target-metric response stability.** For
   \[
   H_G(A)=\frac{\varepsilon}{2}D(A)^\dagger G(A)D(A),
   \qquad g_-I\le G(0)\le g_+I,
   \]
   with exact transverse source \(D^\dagger S=0\), the zero manifold is unchanged and
   \[
   \varepsilon g_-S^\dagger S\le \mathcal C_G
   \le \mathcal K_G(i\zeta)\le \varepsilon g_+S^\dagger S
   \]
   for every finite volume and every \(\zeta>0\). The bounds do not depend on the smallest positive singular value of \(D\).

2. **Gap-independent current \(H_{-1}\) estimate.** For \(G=I+2\eta X\),
   \[
   0\le \mathcal K_G(i\zeta)-\mathcal C_G
   \le \frac{4\varepsilon\eta^2}{g_-}S^\dagger X^\dagger XS.
   \]
   With \(\varepsilon=\lambda^6\Delta\) and \(\eta=\lambda^2\), the static response changes at order \(\lambda^8\Delta\), while the possible temporal defect begins at order \(\lambda^{10}\Delta\).

3. **Two independent remainder sources.** A generic Schrieffer-Wolff remainder produces both a state source \(QRP\) and a current source \(Q(\partial_A R)P\). Current-source control alone is insufficient when the parent pseudoinverse grows.

4. **Sharp anisotropic failure mode.** A bounded local row metric can preserve the zero state while producing a nonzero Drude-Meissner mismatch. The example has bounded but nontight current \(H_{-1}\) weight. Thus row-relative form and a uniform \(H_{-1}\) bound do not by themselves prove the order-of-limits identification.

5. **Isotropic transverse order-of-limits theorem.** Hydrodynamic closure is an operator-theoretic hypothesis: in each small momentum fiber, the cyclic soft subspace generated by the transverse current must admit a momentum-intertwining unitary under which the compressed Hamiltonian, current, and stress share one positive target-metric symbol. The complementary current sector must contribute a jointly continuous common correction bounded by \(O(\varepsilon\eta)\). Under this closure, quasi-locality, a quadratic parent density mode, and a point-group scalar zero-momentum vector symbol,
   \[
   D_K=D_A=D_M
   =\lambda^6\Delta D_{\rm parent}+O(\lambda^8\Delta).
   \]
   No uniform complete many-body gap is assumed. Translation-invariant scalar-convolution row metrics satisfy the closure exactly by Bloch diagonalization, with zero regular correction.

6. **Finite-cluster two-sided row-ideal criterion.** A Hermitian cluster coefficient is state-source safe exactly when
   \[
   r_X=q_Xr_Xq_X=D_X^\dagger Y_XD_X,
   \qquad
   Y_X=(D_X^+)^\dagger r_XD_X^+.
   \]
   For the swap parent, polynomial finite-cluster pseudoinverse growth is compatible with exponential quasi-locality after a small loss of locality exponent.

7. **Complete weighted-degree-eight resolvent class list.** After the two outer weight-two bridge insertions, the only nonzero single-flavor high-resolvent classes of remaining weight four are \(V_1^4\), \(V_{2,Q}^2\), and one matched \(W_4\) insertion. Every \(V_1V_1V_{2,Q}\) ordering vanishes by control-toggle parity, and degree-six vertices cannot enter at total weight eight.

8. **Closed degree-eight source formula.** On the complete product-AGP manifold,
   \[
   R^{(8)}P_{\mathcal Z}
   =\frac{b^2}{\bar\Delta_m^3}
   \left[b^2\mathcal M-\frac{16s^4}{\bar\Delta_s^2}\mathcal B_2\right]P_{\mathcal Z},
   \]
   where
   \[
   \mathcal B_2=\sum_eB_e^2,
   \qquad
   \mathcal M=\sum_eB_e^4+\frac12\sum_{e<f}[B_e,B_f]^\dagger[B_e,B_f].
   \]
   The closed-shell Kramers family replaces \(\mathcal M\) by
   \[
   \mathcal M_\Theta=\frac12\sum_eB_e^4+\frac12\sum_{e<f}[B_e,B_f]^\dagger[B_e,B_f].
   \]

9. **Two-pair all-filling no-go.** On a three-cell two-pair sector,
   \[
   \langle\Phi|\mathcal B_2|0,2\rangle=0,
   \qquad
   \left\langle\Phi\left|\frac12\sum_{e<f}[B_e,B_f]^\dagger[B_e,B_f]\right|0,2\right\rangle=0,
   \]
   while
   \[
   \langle\Phi|\mathcal M|0,2\rangle=8\sqrt3,
   \qquad
   \langle\Phi|\mathcal M_\Theta|0,2\rangle=4\sqrt3.
   \]
   Therefore the unmodified matched single-flavor and closed-shell Kramers families do not preserve the complete product-AGP manifold at weighted degree eight for nonzero bridge amplitude. More precisely, the witness rules out analytic all-composition transfer for every sufficiently small \(\lambda\); it does not rule out an unobserved isolated finite-coupling cancellation involving higher orders.

## Not established

- The original exactly matched finite-coupling microscopic Hamiltonian is not proved to have the dark eigenbranch as its ground state.
- The unmodified model does not preserve the complete product-AGP manifold through weighted degree eight; no all-orders all-composition transfer theorem exists.
- The explicit \(\mathcal C_8\) counterinteraction has not been synthesized solely from the original restricted two-electron exchange/correlated-hopping channels. Its normal ordering contains higher-body active terms.
- No convergent recursive row-ideal completion or all-orders local Schrieffer-Wolff/Feshbach normal form is proved.
- Hydrodynamic closure of the complete operator-valued degree-eight current sector has not been verified for an explicit orbital-level \(d\ge2\) realization. The missing audit is to construct the momentum-fiber cyclic soft subspace, prove its invariance, identify one common compressed \(H/J/T\) symbol, prove that the orthogonal current sector has a jointly continuous \(O(\varepsilon\eta)\) kernel contribution and is uniformly \(H_{-1}\)-tight, and verify the point-group scalar zero-momentum symbol. Therefore the completed degree-eight Kohn-Abelian-Meissner equality remains conditional on that audit.
- The positive-semidefinite dark-branch theorem gives a strict floor for the total Kohn, Abelian, and Meissner kernels. It does not prove equality of the additional gadget contribution in all three limits.
- No material parameter extraction, finite-temperature transition theorem, real-axis conductivity decomposition, or dynamical Maxwell-field screening calculation is claimed.

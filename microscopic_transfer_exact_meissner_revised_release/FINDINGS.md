# Consolidated findings and research decision

## 1. The transfer problem has two distinct objectives

The merged paper separates:

- preserving the **entire degenerate product-AGP composition manifold** under finite-coupling microscopic completion; and
- preserving a **selected coherent paired branch** with a nonzero physical transverse response.

The unmodified matched family fails the first objective at weighted degree eight. A modified positive family already achieves the second objective nonperturbatively.

## 2. The functional-analytic transfer framework is sharp

A small local Schrieffer-Wolff remainder is not enough when the exact parent gap closes. Two sources must be controlled:

\[
QRP,\qquad Q(\partial_A R)P.
\]

The two-sided parent-row ideal removes the state source. Target-metric factorization controls the current source without a uniform gap. The anisotropic counterexample proves that bounded \(H_{-1}\) weight is not the same as soft-tail tightness. Hydrodynamic closure is now formulated operator-theoretically: the momentum-fiber cyclic soft sector generated by the transverse current must admit an intertwiner under which the compressed Hamiltonian, current, and stress share one positive symbol, while the complementary current sector supplies a jointly continuous perturbative correction common to all three limits. Scalar-convolution row metrics satisfy this exactly by Bloch diagonalization, with zero regular correction. Together with point-group scalar zero-momentum structure, this provides the missing condition for

\[
D_K=D_A=D_M.
\]

## 3. The complete degree-eight audit gives a genuine no-go

The full single-flavor source is

\[
R^{(8)}P_{\mathcal Z}
=\frac{b^2}{\bar\Delta_m^3}
\left[b^2\mathcal M-\frac{16s^4}{\bar\Delta_s^2}\mathcal B_2\right]P_{\mathcal Z}.

At two-pair filling, \(\mathcal B_2\) cannot transfer both onsite pairs while \(B_e^4\) can. The explicit witness therefore survives every scalar retuning and the closed-shell Kramers correction. The earlier possibility that unseen degree-eight clusters might cancel the isolated bridge overlap is now closed: the complete retained coefficient is known and is unsafe.

The explicit counterterm

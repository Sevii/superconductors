## Major comments

### M1: two-pair witness proof

Patched Theorem 9.1 with the missing separate statement

\[
\left\langle\Phi\left|\frac12\sum_{e<f}[B_e,B_f]^\dagger[B_e,B_f]\right|0,2\right\rangle=0.
\]

The analytic reason is now explicit: the commutator of number-conserving fermion bilinears is itself a bilinear, so its positive square contains at most two one-electron transfers after normal ordering and cannot move both onsite pairs between blocks. The Kramers value is therefore exactly one half because only the local \(B_e^4\) coefficient is halved. `verify_order8_structure.py` and its retained certificate now check and print the commutator-square matrix element separately.

A scalar-convolution worked example proves the definition exactly by Bloch diagonalization and has zero regular correction.

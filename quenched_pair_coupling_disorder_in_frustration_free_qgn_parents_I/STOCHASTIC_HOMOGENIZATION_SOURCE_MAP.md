## Imported qualitative input

The almost-sure theorem assumes the standard convergence of the periodic cell energy for the explicitly defined space-periodization of a stationary ergodic uniformly elliptic field. The paper states this input as a separate theorem before deriving the QGN stiffness and source-tightness consequences.

## Imported quantitative input

- GNO long version, Theorem 2 under alternative (69b): period-uniform divergence-semigroup decay.
- GNO long version, Remark 11 and equation (70): specialization to the local corrector drift.
- GNO long version, Corollary 8: the low spectral-edge estimate.

Appendix C states these inputs in the paper’s normalization and identifies the volume-independent constants. All subsequent heat-to-tail and QGN formulas are derived in the paper.
